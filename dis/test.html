Instructions

On the next page, you will see four color patches laid out as below, one of which appears to have a different color than the other three. Press the number (1/2/3/4) on your keyboard to indicate the unique one. When uncertain, use Left Arrow and Right Arrow keys to shift the colors to help you. Press the Space key to reset the color shift. You must pick a color (forced choice).

After each trial, you will see random gray dots to reset your visual field, followed by the next trial. The patch with a different color is randomized across trials. Each trial is made to be more challenging if you keep identifying the correct patch, and vice versa. The experiment terminates when a threshold is found.
