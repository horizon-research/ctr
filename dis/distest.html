Instructions

The test consists of a sequence of trials. In each trial, you will be presented with four color patches laid out as below. Your task is to identify the patch whose color differs from the other three. The placement of the differing patch is random.

You can use the Left Arrow and Right Arrow keys to shift the colors of all patches to try and identify the different one. Pressing the Space resets the color shift.

Once you have identified the patch whose color differs from the other three, indicate your choice by pressing one of the four Q/W/A/S letter keys corresponding to the patch's placement (e.g., W in the example below).

S

You must make a choice. Select randomly if you are unable to identify the differing patch.

After each trial, you will see random gray dots to reset your visual field, followed by the next trial.

When ready, press the Enter key to proceed.

Training

The goal is for you to be familiar with the interface. You are encouraged to use the Left Arrow and Right Arrow keys to shift the colors and use the Space key to reset the shift, even though you might not need them to identify the differing patch here.
