Welcome

Thank you for participating in our study. We value your participation and hope that the points below answer some key questions you may have.

What is the goal of our study?

The goal of our project is to develop simple and widely usable assistive technologies for individuals with color vision deficiency (CVD). Eventually, our technologies will help individuals with CVD: 1) better discriminate commonly confusing colors, and 2) communicate in unambiguous color vocabulary with color normal individuals.

Why do you need my participation?

In order to develop our assistive technologies, we need to collect data from individuals who actually suffer from CVD. The data will be collected by asking you to perform a sequence of tasks. The data collected will help improve our internal model and assess the effectiveness of our technologies. Unlike many human labeling tasks that are meant to help train a computer, the tasks you will perform in this study are intended to help CVD individuals like yourself better navigate day to day life.

How long will this study take?

This study consists of a training phase and a test phase. Depending on how much time you want to spend in the training phase, the entire study might take about 5 to 10 minutes.

In what environment should you take the test?

Ideally you take the study in an indoor setting where you can turn off lights and use window blinds to block outdoor lights. Make sure the lighting conditions are roughly the same before and after a break. Throughout the study the monitor should be at a distance of about 80 cm from your eyes, and you should focus your gaze at the center of the monitor. A good test is to fully extend your arm and raise your thumb; the thumb should roughly cover one color patch. The tasks should be performed in full-screen mode, which your browser will automatically enter.
